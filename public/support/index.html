MySurau Support 🕌

Welcome to MySurau — a community-driven app helping Malaysian Muslims find suraus, mosques, and prayer rooms wherever they are.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a surau near me?

Open the app and allow location access when prompted. Suraus near your location appear automatically on the map.

How do I add a new surau that's not on the map?

Long-press any spot on the map and tap "Add Surau". You'll need to be signed in. New suraus added by non-admin users are reviewed before going live.

How do I suggest an edit or report incorrect info?

Open the surau's detail page and use the menu to suggest changes, report that the surau doesn't exist, or flag it as permanently closed.

Why don't I see prayer times?

Prayer times come from JAKIM's e-Solat API based on your state. Make sure location permission is enabled — without it we can't determine which JAKIM zone to use.

How do I sign in or create an account?

Open the menu (top right) → "Sign up or log in". You can use Sign in with Apple, Google, or email + password.

Can I use the app without signing in?

Yes. You can browse the map, view surau details, and check prayer times without an account. Sign-in is only required to add or edit suraus.

How do I enable prayer time notifications?

Open the menu (top right) and toggle on "Prayer alert". You'll be asked to allow notifications.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete account". This removes your account and stops linking your contributions to you.
